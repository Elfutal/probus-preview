What we do

Our services

Practical, independent advice that gives clients, contractors and joint ventures confidence in their numbers.

  • Budget advice and cost plans

    Budget advice for infrastructure projects, including setting up cost plans.

  • Risk management

    Identifying commercial risk early and managing it through the project.

  • Quantification and estimating

    Accurate measurement of quantities and cost estimates.

  • Independent cost advice and forensic audits

    Impartial cost advice and forensic audits for joint ventures and partnerships.

  • Early contractor involvement (ECI)

    Advice during the ECI phase, including assessment of prices and best value.

    Procurement advice and the preparation of procurement documentation.

  • Tender analysis and evaluation

    Structured analysis and evaluation of tender returns, so awards rest on a sound basis.
